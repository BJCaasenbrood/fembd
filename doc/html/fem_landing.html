fem: Linear-Elastic Assemblies

fem builds linear-elastic finite-element models of assemblies of STL parts. Each part is meshed with Gmsh (quadratic Tet10 or linear Tet4 elements) or treated as rigid. Parts are connected by bonded ties, elastic interfaces, couplings and joints. The result is the sparse stiffness matrix K and mass matrix M, ready for modal, harmonic, transient or reduced-order analysis.

steel = fem.material(210e9, 0.3, 7850);
arm   = fem.Body('arm.stl', steel, Size = 4, Scale = 1e-3);
a = fem.Assembly();  a.add(arm);
a.fix('arm', @(X) X(:,3) < 1e-9);
[K, M, T] = a.system();          % u = T*q
[V, f] = fem.modes(K, M, 10);    % natural frequencies (Hz)

Features

  • STL import and Gmsh meshing (Tet4/Tet10), ASCII .msh 2.2/4.1 import
  • isotropic and anisotropic materials; consistent or HRZ-lumped mass
  • elastic and rigid bodies; rigid bodies from STL surfaces or mass properties only
  • bonded ties and elastic interfaces between non-matching meshes
  • rigid couplings and kinematic joints (revolute, prismatic, spherical, ...) with springs, closed loops, and finite posing of joints
  • exact constraint elimination u = T*q; modes of free systems; Craig-Bampton and Guyan reduction
