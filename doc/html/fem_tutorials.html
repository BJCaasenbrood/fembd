Tutorials

Runnable scripts that explain the toolbox step by step. Each one builds its own geometry and, where possible, checks its results against theory. The source files are in the tutorials folder; run them section by section in the Editor.

  1. Your First Model: the workflow from STL to modes, checked against Euler-Bernoulli
  2. Meshing: meshSTL options, Tet4 vs Tet10 convergence, Gmsh GUI meshes, Scale and transform
  3. Materials and Mass: orthotropic materials, mass properties from M, consistent vs lumped mass
  4. Ties and Interfaces: non-matching meshes, bonded ties, Tol, partial bonding, elastic interfaces
  5. Loads and Statics: dofs, supports, point loads, gravity, reactions
  6. Rigid Bodies: reference dofs, tip masses, RefPoint, mesh-free rigid bodies
  7. Couplings and Joints: joint types, springs, locking, mechanism modes, posing, closed loops, belt transmissions
  8. Dynamics: Rayleigh damping, frequency response, Newmark time integration, state space
  9. Model Reduction: Guyan and Craig-Bampton, recovering the full field
